Soli Deo Gloria — A Dedication

All glory to God.

God made everything that is. Nothing any of us makes is made from
nothing: we work with what we were given, with hands and minds we did not
make, in a world we did not build. What we call creating is arranging,
and what we arrange was never ours.

To put our name on the result and call it property is to claim what was
never ours to claim. So the author of this work claims no ownership of it
and asks nothing for it — not as a reproach to anyone who holds
otherwise, nor as a claim to have seen the matter more clearly than they
have, but as the plainest thing one person can do about something
received.

This conviction is older and wider than the words at the head of this
page, which were first written at the end of a page of music. It belongs
to anyone who holds that the world was given and not earned, by whatever
name they know the One who gave it.

The law has no way to record "this was never mine." What follows is the
nearest instrument it provides, included so that this dedication takes
effect in the courts of every country and not only on paper.

In plain terms, adding nothing to and subtracting nothing from the text
below: anyone may copy, change, publish, perform, sell, or build upon
this work, for any purpose, without permission and without payment.
