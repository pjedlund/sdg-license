# Soli Deo Gloria — A Public Domain Dedication

![Calligraphic monogram of the letters S, D, and G, intertwined with ornate flourishes and a sweeping underline.](logo/SDG-logo.webp "Official Soli Deo Gloria logo")

SDG is a narrative wrapper over the Creative Commons CC0 1.0 Universal Public Domain Dedication. The operative legal text of any SDG-dedicated work is CC0 1.0 verbatim. SDG adds two short paragraphs of framing — drawn from the tradition of Johann Sebastian Bach and Leo Tolstoy of giving work freely rather than holding it — but it does not change CC0's legal effect. SDG is the name of this wrapper project, not a license identifier; files dedicated under SDG remain `SPDX-License-Identifier: CC0-1.0`.

## Who SDG is for

Authors, artists, researchers, editors, and others publishing prose, art, scholarship, data, or cultural-heritage material who want the broadest dedication to the worldwide public domain that the law presently allows, and who want that dedication framed by a short statement of intent rather than presented as a bare legal document. If you would otherwise have published your work under CC0 directly, SDG gives you the same legal effect with a preamble.

## Who SDG is not for

Patent-sensitive code. CC0 1.0 does not waive patent rights, and Fedora stopped accepting CC0 for code in 2022 for that reason. If you are publishing source code where patent exposure is a real concern, use Apache 2.0 outright (which has an explicit patent grant) or MIT-0, or pair SDG with Apache 2.0 by dual-licensing. See [docs/patents-and-code.md](docs/patents-and-code.md).

Projects that require a clean, complete waiver of moral rights. No public-domain dedication achieves this in jurisdictions where moral rights are inalienable, including France, Germany, Italy, Spain, and many others. CC0's fallback construction is designed to come as close as the law allows, but it is honest about its ceiling. See [docs/moral-rights.md](docs/moral-rights.md).

## Origin

SDG was first written for and adopted by the [tolstoy.life](https://tolstoy.life/) project, where the Bach and Tolstoy framing was a natural fit for an open scholarship project rooted in Tolstoy's own renunciation of copyright. SDG is now maintained as a neutral, standalone project so any third party can adopt it without inheriting tolstoy.life branding. Tolstoy is the first adopter and the origin of the wrapper text, not the home of the project.

## Reusing SDG in your own project

Anyone may adopt SDG by copying the `LICENSE` file from this repository into the root of their own project. The name "SDG" and the wrapper text may be reused as-is. If you modify the wrapper text in ways that change its meaning, you must not call your modified version "SDG" or any close variant — see [docs/trademark.md](docs/trademark.md). The operative CC0 1.0 text in `LICENSE` must remain verbatim; do not edit it. If you'd like to propose changes to the wrapper text, docs, or examples, see [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md).
